Frequently Asked Questions

What area of specialization does Dr. Vikas Gupta have? expand_more

Dr. Gupta specializes in neurovascular surgery (both microsurgical and endovascular/neuro-intervention), acute stroke and hemorrhagic/ischemic stroke interventions, aneurysm treatment (endovascular coiling & microsurgery), AVM embolization, neuro-oncology (brain and spine tumors), minimally invasive spine surgery and spine reconstructions, microsurgery for brain tumors, and microdiscectomy.

Does Dr. Vikas Gupta offer Telemedicine Consultation? expand_more

Yes, Dr. Vikas Gupta provides telemedicine consultations, allowing patients to connect with a top neurosurgeon in Delhi from the comfort of their home.

How many years of experience does Dr. Vikas Gupta have? expand_more

Dr. Vikas Gupta has over 30 years of experience in neurosurgery, treating complex brain and spine conditions with advanced surgical techniques.

What are the common conditions treated by Dr. Vikas Gupta? expand_more

Dr. Vikas Gupta treats a wide range of neurological conditions, including brain stroke, brain tumors, spinal disc diseases, neurovascular disorders, hydrocephalus, pediatric neurosurgical conditions, spinal deformities, and geriatric neurological issues.
