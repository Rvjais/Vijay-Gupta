Symptoms

Recognize Brain Hemorrhage Warning Signs

Early recognition can save lives. Seek immediate medical attention if you experience these symptoms.

Sudden Severe Headache

Often described as the "worst headache of your life"

Weakness or Numbness

Especially on one side of the body (face, arm, or leg)

Vision Problems

Blurred vision, double vision, or loss of vision

Speech Difficulties

Slurred speech, difficulty speaking, or understanding

Loss of Consciousness

Fainting, confusion, or seizures

Nausea and Vomiting

Especially when combined with severe headache
